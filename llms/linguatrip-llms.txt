# Linguatrip

> Linguatrip is a language-education and language-travel company that sells two
> connected things: bookings for accredited language schools abroad (with
> accommodation, transfers and visa support) and its own online English school
> with self-paced courses, live tutoring and exam preparation. Founded in 2014
> and headquartered in the United States, it is backed by 500 Global.

Linguatrip does not publish a public API, developer portal, SDKs or MCP server.
The surfaces below are consumer web properties. This file is generated by the
API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the catalog entry at apis.yml; it is not
published by Linguatrip.

## Booking marketplace

- [Language courses abroad](https://linguatrip.com/en/english/): Search and book English language courses at schools abroad, filtered by country, city, school, course type and dates. Prices are quoted in a user-selected currency from a list of 18.
- [Study in the United Kingdom](https://linguatrip.com/en/english/united_kingdom/london/): Country- and city-level school listings, the deepest part of the catalog.
- [Study in Malta](https://linguatrip.com/en/english/malta/): Country-level school listings.
- [Study in Dubai](https://linguatrip.com/en/english/united_arab_emirates/dubai/): City-level school listings.
- [Higher education abroad](https://linguatrip.com/en/higher_education): University placement consulting for the United States, United Kingdom and Canada, including application and motivation-letter services.

## Online school

- [Linguatrip online school](https://edu.linguatrip.com/): The learning platform hosting purchased courses. Sign-in is passwordless — an email address receives a verification code.
- [Private lessons](https://online.linguatrip.com/private_lessons): One-to-one tutoring sessions.
- [Level test](https://online.linguatrip.com/leveltest): Free English placement test.
- Course catalog: 25+ programs spanning level progression (elementary through proficiency), speaking and pronunciation, grammar, business and workplace English, kids' English, and exam preparation for IELTS, TOEFL, GMAT, GRE and SAT.
